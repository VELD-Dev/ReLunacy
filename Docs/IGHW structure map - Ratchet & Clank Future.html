IGHW structure map

Ratchet & Clank Future level files, as read by ReLunacy. Each card is a binary structure (big-endian) with its field offsets; the bar under the title shows byte occupancy. Links start at the referencing field and end at the referenced structure.

Click a structure header to isolate its incoming and outgoing links; click again (or press Esc) to reset. Hover a linked field to highlight its link. The map scrolls horizontally.

Source: code of VELD-Dev/ReLunacy ([FileOffset] attributes and manual reads in the readers). Only fields read by the tool are named; the rest is marked “unk”. Names and roles are the repository’s, not an official Insomniac specification.

Structures shared between engines (MobyBangle, MobyMesh, MobySkeleton, MobyBone, TiePrimitiveBounds, vertex formats): same layout, but some fields are interpreted differently (absolute offset vs. section-relative offset, direct index vs. index into a TUID table).
