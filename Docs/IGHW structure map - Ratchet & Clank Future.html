Ratchet & Clank Future level files, as read by ReLunacy. Each card is a binary structure (big-endian) with its field offsets; the bar under the title shows byte occupancy. Links start at the referencing field and end at the referenced structure.

Click a structure header to isolate its incoming and outgoing links; click again (or press Esc) to reset. Hover a linked field to highlight its link. The map scrolls horizontally.

Source: code of VELD-Dev/ReLunacy at tag 0.06-alpha.1 (commit a906962) ([FileOffset] attributes and manual reads in the readers). Only fields read by the tool are named; the rest is marked “unk”. Names and roles are the repository’s, not an official Insomniac specification.

Not shown: the Lua scripts edited by 0.06 live in global_cached.psarc (indexed by game.toc), outside the IGHW level files. Labels such as INFERRED or STRONG in notes repeat the confidence level stated in the ReLunacy source.

Collision (dev): collision.dat on Tools of Destruction / Quest for Booty is a raw big-endian blob, not IGHW; the new engine wraps the same blob in section 0x2900 of each region’s collision.dat. The ToD layout was first decoded by @sowelilili (ReLunacy-ToD-tools); the new-engine mesh, instance order and primitive sets were decoded for ReLunacy. Evidence and open questions: docs/reverse/collision.md.

Structures shared between engines (MobyBangle, MobyMesh, MobySkeleton, MobyBone, TiePrimitiveBounds, vertex formats): same layout, but some fields are interpreted differently (absolute offset vs. section-relative offset, direct index vs. index into a TUID table).
